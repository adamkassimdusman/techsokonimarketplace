# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — current development milestone
Working local vertical slices: catalogue, accounts, listings/photos, favourites, conversations/offers, inspection requests, moderation, profiles, super-admin access management and private client support. This is not the entire acceptance-ready Phase 1.

Next: implement confirmed email ownership/admin MFA, complete message and support history pagination, listing attributes/filters, production authentication/storage and full workflow E2E tests; validate external PostgreSQL; admin pagination and search; review accessible comparison UX; safe operational rollout.

## Phase 2
Real provider-backed seller verification, inspector role/assignments/checklists, approval/certificates/QR and revocation, safe meeting workflows, dealer storefronts, configurable inspection packages/promotions, eligible reviews and consignment. Never award verification based on payment.

## Phase 3
Provider-approved marketplace payments, orders, commissions, delivery, refunds/disputes and settlement. No escrow wallet. Authenticated/idempotent callback ledger, sandbox tests and approval before production activation.

## Phase 4
Inventory-grounded AI search/pricing, transparent recommendations, moderated fraud flags, buyer requests, bulk imports, trade-ins and regional expansion.


## Premium redesign stages
See REDESIGN_PROGRESS.md for feature-level status. Current core delivery covers foundation and core marketplace visuals, with substantial comparison/gallery/dealer/messaging/draft/photo work and initial rules-based discovery. Next: structured product attributes and richer owner-managed storefronts, then real inspection reports/certificates and configurable service packages. Saved-search event notifications, price-change history, privacy-conscious analytics and PWA are later stages; do not fabricate these capabilities.
